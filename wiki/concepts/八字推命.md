# 八字推命 (Bazi / Four Pillars of Destiny)

Traditional Chinese fate-calculation system based on a person's birth datetime, encoded as eight characters (八字) across four pillars (四柱).

## Core Structure

| Pillar | Maps to | Heavenly Stem (天干) | Earthly Branch (地支) |
|--------|---------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| Year (年柱) | Ancestry, social context | 1 char | 1 char |
| Month (月柱) | Parents, early life | 1 char | 1 char |
| Day (日柱) | Self (日主) | 1 char | 1 char |
| Hour (時柱) | Children, late life | 1 char | 1 char |

- **10 Heavenly Stems** (甲乙丙丁戊己庚辛壬癸): paired Wood/Fire/Earth/Metal/Water × Yin/Yang
- **12 Earthly Branches** (子丑寅卯...): 12 zodiac animals; each contains hidden stems
- **Five Elements (五行)**: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — production (生) and control (剋) cycles drive interpretation

## Temporal Reasoning Components

- **大運 (Major Luck Pillar)**: 10-year cycles determining life phase; calculated from birth + gender
- **流年 (Annual Pillar)**: yearly overlay; interactions with natal chart drive year-specific predictions
- **六合/三合/沖/刑**: combination and clash patterns between branches — trigger events

## Why It Challenges LLMs

Computing a Bazi chart requires exact **calendar arithmetic** (Gregorian → Chinese lunisolar + sexagenary cycle), then applying hundreds of interaction rules compositionally across multiple temporal layers. See [[BaziQA-Benchmark]] for how this is formalized as an LLM evaluation task.
